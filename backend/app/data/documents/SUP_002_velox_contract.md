# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT: VELOX SEMICONDUCTOR ASIA
**Document ID:** MSA-2024-VELOX-002  
**Version:** 1.8 (Active)  
**Effective Date:** March 01, 2024  
**Expiration Date:** February 28, 2027  
**Supplier Tier:** Tier-2 Semiconductor & Integrated Sensor Specialist  
**Registered Facility:** Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan / Assembly Center in Penang, Malaysia  

---

### Section 1: Scope of Supply
Velox Semiconductor Asia ("Supplier") manufactures and supplies wearable biometric sensors, Bluetooth 5.4 transceiver chips, and power management ICs (PMICs).

### Section 2: Order Terms & Lead Times
2.1 **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** 300 units per line item.  
2.2 **Standard International Lead Time:** **18 calendar days** for sea-air intermodal transit to Indian fulfillment centers (Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Delhi).  
2.3 **Express Production:** Lead time can be reduced to **9 calendar days** upon payment of an expedited fee of 30% of order value and direct international air courier dispatch.

### Section 3: Inbound Customs and Freight Allocation
3.1 Delivery terms are Delivered At Place (DAP - Incoterms 2020) at Buyer's specified regional distribution warehouse.  
3.2 In case of international port congestion exceeding 72 hours, Supplier is obligated to notify Buyer within 4 hours and submit revised flight manifest tracking details.

### Section 4: Lead-Time Variance and Buffer Stock Program
4.1 Supplier covenants to hold minimum buffer stock of 1,000 units of primary biometric sensors in Penang regional warehouse.  
4.2 When Buyer issues an "Emergency Stockout Call-off", buffer units must be dispatched within 24 hours via express DHL/FedEx cargo.
