# CORPORATE PROCUREMENT POLICY & DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY (DOA)
**Document Ref:** SOP-PROC-2025-V3.2  
**Department:** Global Supply Chain & Strategic Procurement  
**Classification:** Internal Operational Policy  
**Effective Date:** November 01, 2025  

---

### 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy governs all purchasing decisions, supplier awards, inventory replenishment thresholds, and emergency orders for finished consumer hardware and spare components.

### 2. Financial Delegation of Authority (Approval Matrix)
Every Purchase Order (PO) or replenishment proposal must obtain written electronic approval based on the financial commitment thresholds below:

| Spend Tier (INR) | Primary Authority Required | Secondary Co-Signer | Standard SLA for Approval |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: < ₹1,50,000** | Inventory Analyst / Replenishment Lead | Automated System Validation | < 2 Hours |
| **Tier 2: ₹1,50,000 – ₹7,50,000** | Supply Chain Operations Manager | None required | < 4 Hours |
| **Tier 3: ₹7,50,001 – ₹25,00,000** | Head of Global Procurement | Finance Controller | < 8 Hours |
| **Tier 4: > ₹25,00,000** | Vice President of Supply Chain | Chief Operating Officer (COO) | < 24 Hours |

### 3. Sole-Source and Emergency Purchase Policy
3.1 **Emergency Declaration Criteria:** An emergency purchase may be authorized if:  
   - Forecasted stockout of an "A-Class" revenue product will occur in less than 7 calendar days.  
   - Potential lost sales exceed ₹3,00,000.  
   - Existing primary supplier has issued a formal delay notification exceeding 5 days.  
3.2 **Expedited Secondary Sourcing:** The Procurement Team is authorized to place orders with secondary pre-qualified vendors at up to a **15% price premium** without requiring VP approval, provided the total order does not exceed ₹10,00,000.

### 4. Working Capital and Inventory Budget Constraints
4.1 The total monthly replenishment spend across all fulfillment centers must not exceed the quarterly budget allocation approved by Corporate Finance (Current Cap: ₹85,00,000 per fiscal quarter).  
4.2 Warehouses with current inventory utilization exceeding 85% volumetric capacity cannot receive non-critical replenishments without prior stock transfer or promotional liquidation.
