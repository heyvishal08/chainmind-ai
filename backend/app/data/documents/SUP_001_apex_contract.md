# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT: APEX MICROTRONICS LTD & CHAINMIND TECH
**Document ID:** MSA-2024-APEX-001  
**Version:** 2.4 (Active)  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Expiration Date:** December 31, 2026  
**Supplier Tier:** Tier-1 Strategic Electronic Component Supplier  
**Registered Facility:** Chakan Industrial Corridor, Pune, Maharashtra, India  

---

### Section 1: Scope of Supply and Product Catalog
Apex Microtronics Ltd ("Supplier") agrees to manufacture, test, package, and deliver electronic modules, wireless chips, and sub-assemblies ("Products") specified in Purchase Orders issued by ChainMind Tech ("Buyer").

### Section 2: Standard Order Terms & Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)
2.1 **Base Minimum Order Quantity:** The standard MOQ for smart audio micro-controllers and wireless chipsets is 150 units per SKU per Purchase Order.  
2.2 **Standard Lead Time:** Standard production and dispatch lead time is **12 calendar days** from electronic receipt and confirmation of the Purchase Order.  
2.3 **Lead Time Variability Tolerance:** The Supplier covenants that 95% of orders shall be fulfilled within ±2 days of standard lead time.

### Section 3: Pricing and Volume Rebates
3.1 Base contract pricing is fixed annually in Indian Rupees (INR) as outlined in Schedule B.  
3.2 **Volume Discounts:**  
- Orders between 500 – 1,499 units: 4.5% discount on total unit procurement cost.  
- Orders exceeding 1,500 units: 8.0% discount on total unit procurement cost.

### Section 4: Late Delivery Penalties and Liquidated Damages
4.1 If the Supplier fails to deliver the specified quantity by the Confirmed Delivery Date due to causes solely attributable to the Supplier:  
- **Grace Period:** A 24-hour grace period is granted.  
- **Daily Penalty:** 1.5% of the total Purchase Order value per day of delay, capped at a maximum of 15% of the PO value.  
4.2 **Chronic Delay Breach:** Failure to deliver within seven (7) business days of the Confirmed Delivery Date grants the Buyer the legal right to cancel the Purchase Order without cancellation charges and procure substitute inventory from secondary vendors.

### Section 5: Expedited Delivery and Emergency Orders
5.1 **Emergency Fast-Track Production:** The Buyer may request expedited manufacturing with a shortened lead time of **5 calendar days**.  
5.2 Expedited production incurs a **22% surcharge** on base unit cost.  
5.3 Expedited air freight expenses shall be borne by the Buyer unless the expedition is necessitated by previous unfulfilled Supplier backlog, in which case the Supplier pays 100% of expedited freight costs.

### Section 6: Quality Standards and Defect Thresholds
6.1 Defect rate must not exceed 1.2% measured upon inbound warehouse inspection. Batches exceeding 2.0% defective units shall trigger a full quarantine, and Supplier must replace defective lots within 96 hours at their sole expense.
