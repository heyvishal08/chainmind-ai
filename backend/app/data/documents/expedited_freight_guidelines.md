# EXPEDITED FREIGHT & LOGISTICS SLA GUIDELINES
**Document Ref:** SOP-LOG-2025-04  
**Department:** Global Logistics & Fulfillment  
**Effective Date:** January 10, 2025  

---

### 1. Objective
To establish quantitative criteria for authorizing premium expedited freight (Air Cargo / Dedicated Express Truckload) versus standard ground intermodal transportation.

### 2. Economic Justification Test (Cost-Benefit Ratio)
Expedited freight shall only be authorized if the projected financial benefit satisfies the following formula:
$$\text{Net Benefit} = (\text{Estimated Lost Gross Margin from Stockout}) - (\text{Expedited Freight Premium}) > 0$$

- If **Net Benefit > ₹50,000**, expedited freight is strongly recommended.
- If **Net Benefit is between ₹0 and ₹50,000**, approval from the Supply Chain Operations Manager is required.
- If **Net Benefit < ₹0**, expedited freight is strictly prohibited unless protecting a contractual Key Enterprise Customer SLA with penalty clauses.

### 3. Transit Time Targets by Transport Mode
- **Air Freight (BlueDart Apex / SpiceXpress):** 24 to 36 hours domestic hub-to-hub. Cost factor: 3.8x base surface rate.
- **Dedicated Express Road Freight (Delhivery Prime):** 48 to 72 hours domestic cross-corridor (e.g., Delhi to Bengaluru / Mumbai to Chennai). Cost factor: 1.6x base surface rate.
- **Standard Ground Intermodal:** 5 to 8 business days. Base cost benchmark.

### 4. Carrier Selection Hierarchy
1. Primary Carrier: BlueDart Express (Contract SLA: 96.2% On-Time Delivery).
2. Secondary Carrier: Delhivery Prime Logistics (Contract SLA: 93.5% On-Time Delivery).
3. Heavy Bulk Carrier: Gati KWE / VRL Logistics (Contract SLA: 89.0% On-Time Delivery, suitable for palletized transfers).
